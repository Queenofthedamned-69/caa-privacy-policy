Privacy Policy

Crimes Against Apathy
Operated by Crimes Against Apathy LLC, Napoleon, Ohio
Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Crimes Against Apathy Android app handles personal information. It applies to the app and to this page. Contact: juliekatakis@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account. Email address and password so you can create an account and sign in. The password is handled by our authentication provider. We do not ask you to send your password by email.
  • Incident reports. The description you type, the type of incident, and an approximate place. The app does not share your precise live location with other users.
  • Teams. Notes, availability, and calendar entries you add to a team. Other members of that team can see those shared entries.
  • Support. The subject and message if you write to the Crimes Against Apathy support team.
  • On your device. The app may store preferences and a local copy of your own records on the phone or tablet.

Information we do not collect

The app does not use an advertising ID. It does not collect health information, payment card numbers, bank information, or precise live location to share with other users. We do not sell personal information.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, store the reports and team notes you choose to file, show shared team entries to members of that team, reply to support messages, and keep the service secure. We do not use it for advertising.

Who receives it

Account and app data are stored with our service provider, Supabase, which hosts the database and sign-in system. Team members can see entries shared inside their own team. We may disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell it to data brokers or advertisers.

Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

How long we keep it

We keep account and report information while your account exists, unless you ask us to delete it or we must keep a limited record to meet a legal duty.

Your choices

Email juliekatakis@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account and the reports tied to it. The app does not currently let you delete stored reports while keeping the account.

Age

Crimes Against Apathy is for people 18 and over. We do not aim the app at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
